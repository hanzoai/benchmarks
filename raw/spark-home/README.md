# raw/spark-home — recovered benchmark records

Forty-seven benchmark logs, scripts and outputs recovered from the home directory of
the spark GPU box, where they were the ONLY copy. They are the raw measurements behind
the ROCm/Vulkan kernel work: decode and prefill sweeps, dtype baselines, flash-attention
arms, and the cell A/B runs.

They are here so they are versioned and mirrored rather than resident on one disk.
They are NOT the final form. Each of these should become an experiment on the evidence
plane — a hypothesis, the arms, and a verdict — recorded through the SDK rather than
parsed back out of a log. This directory shrinks as that happens.
